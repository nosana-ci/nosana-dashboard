Zephyr is a series of language models that are trained to act as helpful assistants. Zephyr 141B-A35B is the latest model in the series, and is a fine-tuned version of [Mixtral 8x22b](https://ollama.com/library/mixtral:8x22b).

## Sizes

- `zephyr:141b`: A Mixture of Experts (MoE) model with 141B total parameters and 35B active parameters.
- `zephyr:7b`: The original Zephyr model
