Nemotron-Cascade-2-30B-A3B is an open 30B MoE model with 3B activated parameters that delivers strong reasoning and agentic capabilities. It achieves gold medal performance in both the 2025 International Mathematical Olympiad (IMO) and the International Olympiad in Informatics (IOI). It operates in both thinking and instruct (non-reasoning) modes.
